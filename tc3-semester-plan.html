INDIVIDUAL · GRADED

3-minute talk

  • Slides, no text on them
  • No written notes
  • 2h prep, then present
  • 3 minutes, timed
  • Answer questions
Continuous assessment
3-MINUTE TALK · RECIPE

Building good slides

  • One idea per slide
  • Images and data, not sentences
  • Consistent colors and layout
  • Readable from the back of the room
  • Rehearse without notes
3-MINUTE TALK · DELIVERY

Delivering it on the day

  • No notes, no reading
  • Eye contact, not the screen
  • Keep to 3 minutes
  • Know your content cold
  • Anticipate questions
Practice out loud, without notes, before the exam
FINAL EVALUATION

Cover letter

  • Answer a job ad
  • Fits your engineering specialty
  • Written, individual
