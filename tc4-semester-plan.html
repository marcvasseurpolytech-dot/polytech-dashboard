Research poster

  • Design a scientific poster, in pairs
  • 2h prep, then present
  • Title, intro, key findings, visuals
  • Defend it orally
  • Answer questions
Mid-semester · continuous assessment
RESEARCH POSTER · RECIPE

Building a good poster

  • Title, intro, method, results, conclusion
  • Grid layout, consistent colors
  • Few words, strong visuals
  • Readable from 2 meters
  • Rehearse your oral defense
Subject: cross-cutting if different departments, department-specific otherwise
RESEARCH POSTER · THE DEFENSE

What you actually do

  • Stand next to your poster
  • 2 to 3 min guided walkthrough
  • Point to each section as you speak
  • Invite questions, then answer
  • Both partners take part
RESEARCH POSTER · ORAL DEFENSE

Defending it on the day

  • Don't read the poster aloud
  • Explain in your own words
  • Eye contact, not the wall
  • Know your data cold
  • Anticipate questions
  • Both partners speak
Practice out loud, in pairs, before the exam
FINAL EVALUATION

Text summary

  • Summarize a text
  • Your own words, own structure
  • Written, individual
